# AI Usage Disclosure

This document explains how AI was used to build [Quantum Smart Wallet](https://github.com/andreysobol/quantum-smart-wallet) and, just as importantly, which parts were written by humans.

**The core idea, cryptography, signature verification, and security-critical smart-contract and account-abstraction code are human-designed and human-written.** We used AI extensively to help turn that core into a usable, publicly deployed demo.

## Human-crafted core

The following work was designed and implemented by humans, not generated by AI:

- **Protocol design and security model:** the idea of a post-quantum Ethereum smart wallet that authorizes operations with hash-based signatures.
- **Post-quantum cryptography:** the Rust implementation of SLH-DSA and its key generation, signing, and verification logic in [`rslh/`](rslh/).
- **On-chain signature verification:** the Solidity SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s verifier in [`slhsol/src/SlhDsaSha2_128s.sol`](slhsol/src/SlhDsaSha2_128s.sol).
- **Native account abstraction:** the quantum account, factory, and frame-transaction support in [`slhsol/src/`](slhsol/src/), including the human-written logic for validating and authorizing transactions under [EIP-8141](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8141).
- **Security-critical smart contracts:** the wallet authorization and related core contract logic, including the quantum-gated Uniswap v4 hook.

We implemented against existing standards and proposals, including [NIST FIPS 205](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.205.pdf), EIP-8141, and [ERC-8421](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8421-frame-transaction-alternative-mempools/29800). We do not claim to have authored those standards or proposals.

## AI-assisted development

AI was used as an engineering and production tool for the work surrounding the human-written core, including:

- **DevNet infrastructure:** configuring and deploying the Ethereum DevNet, node services, alternative mempool, RPC gateway, explorer, and related operational tooling in [`infra/`](infra/).
- **Deployment and automation:** scripts, service configuration, CI workflows, deployment plumbing, and integration of existing components into a working demo.
- **Frontend and application delivery:** building and deploying the web wallet, integrating existing cryptographic and contract interfaces, and implementing user-facing flows in [`apps/web/`](apps/web/).
- **Design and presentation:** UI styling, visual assets, layouts, and demo presentation.
- **Documentation and copy:** README files, setup instructions, explanations, and other text content.
- **Supporting tools and tests:** assistance with integration scripts, testing infrastructure, and developer tooling outside the human-crafted cryptographic and security-critical core.

AI assistance did **not** replace the human design and implementation of the signature scheme, on-chain verifier, or account-abstraction authorization logic. In particular, running an alternative mempool or deploying a DevNet is infrastructure work; defining the wallet's cryptographic authorization rules is core protocol work.

## Why we made this distinction

We believe AI is useful for accelerating integration, infrastructure, deployment, and product development. But for this project, the parts that define **what authorizes a transaction and why a signature is valid** were deliberately hand-crafted by humans so the code and its security assumptions could be understood, reasoned about, and reviewed directly.

This is a disclosure of our development process, **not a security certification**. Quantum Smart Wallet remains research and educational software, has not been audited, and must not be used to protect real funds.
